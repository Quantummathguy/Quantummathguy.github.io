About Me

A brief introduction to my academic background and current research interests.

I completed my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics at the University of Arizona under the guidance of Dr. Janek Wehr.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Universidad CEU Cardenal Herrera and Universitat Politècnica de València.

My research interests lie primarily at the intersection of applied mathematics, quantum information, and mathematical physics.

Outside of research, I enjoy spending time with family, learning languages, running, and skateboarding.

Research

My work is centered on mathematical and physical aspects of quantum systems.

Open Quantum Systems

Mathematical models describing quantum systems interacting with their environment, including stochastic and dissipative dynamics.

Quantum Information

Quantum information and computation, with an emphasis on mathematical structures underlying quantum systems.

Mathematical Physics

Operator theory, stochastic processes, quantum optics, quantum foundations, and quantum many-body physics.
